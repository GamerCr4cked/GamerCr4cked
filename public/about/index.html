I'm GamerCr4cked, from Bangladesh. I'm studying in the vocational education track and spend much of my free time exploring technology, gaming, and creative projects. I'm not presenting myself as an expert — I'm learning by trying things, solving problems, and finishing projects.

A LITTLE BACKGROUND

Technology is my main interest.

I enjoy understanding how websites and software work, then applying what I learn to something of my own. My web projects have introduced me to HTML, CSS, JavaScript, GitHub, and Cloudflare Pages. I'm also learning Java because I'd like to understand programming more deeply and eventually create Minecraft plugins.

Minecraft is another big part of what I enjoy. I like the technical side of it — setting up servers, thinking about gameplay systems, and building experiences that friends can play together.

I also enjoy anime, manga, and light novels. That interest led me to work on Tidewritten, a reading website where I can combine web development with stories and multilingual presentation.
